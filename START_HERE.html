Deformation Monitoring and Analysis

Theory, Methods and Practice for Surveyors and Engineers

A Practical Guide with Worked Examples and Interactive Apps

Yam Khoon Tor, PhD, HFSISV

These are the companion apps of the book, for use on your own computer without an internet connection. Each opens with the book’s data already entered: repeat any worked example, then change the numbers to see how the results respond. Choose an app below.

Start here

One Network Explorer

The book’s teaching network followed through every stage: adjustment and datum, outliers and robust estimation, two-epoch deformation analysis and many-epoch monitoring with the Kalman filter. A good place to start.

 Part I

Part I Solver

Foundations: The movement test, error ellipses, datum and redundancy, the levelling loop, mean and standard deviation, 2D displacement tests, polar propagation, required precision, the global test and the null-space matrix.

 Part II

Part II Solver

Least squares adjustment: Levelling weights, the adjustment of any small levelling network, redundancy and condition equations, sequential updates with the innovation test, linearization coefficients and variance component estimation.

 Part III

Part III Solver

The datum problem: Datum defects, S-transformations for levelling, a two-epoch explorer that shows the running example in any datum (and can generate new epochs from any movements you choose), and the congruency test.

 Part IV

Part IV Solver

Quality and outliers: Minimal detectable biases, a network analyzer in which you add blunders to any line and compare data snooping, L1, Huber, IGG III and the Danish method, the w-test, weight functions, mean versus median, and the smallest detectable movement.

 Part V

Part V Solver

Static deformation analysis: Pooled variance factors, the global congruency test, single-point elimination round by round, IWST, strain analysis and building tilt with angular distortion.

 Part VI

Part VI Solver

Kinematic and dynamic analysis: Kinematic models and predictions, the standard, robust and adaptive Kalman filters week by week, the HST dam model with the full 48-month data set, correlated noise, the bias from a missing annual term, and traffic-light alarms.

 Part VII

Part VII Solver

Related estimation techniques: Collocation along a line, total versus ordinary least squares with a Monte Carlo simulation, ridge regularization and the Bayesian test.

 Part VIII

Part VIII Solver

Sensors, practice and case studies: The laser-scanning level of detection, InSAR line-of-sight conversion, network pre-analysis (Designs A, B and C, or your own levelling network), epoch frequency, tunnel convergence and statistical tables on demand.

 Appendix E

Appendix E Solver

Generalized inverses: Generalized inverses of any levelling network in any datum, the null-space matrix with orientation unknowns, configuration defects and the rank-tolerance pitfall.
All calculations run in your web browser on this computer; no data are sent anywhere. Keep all the files of this folder together. Online versions of the same apps are linked from the book.
