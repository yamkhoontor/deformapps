Part III Solver

Calculators for the Part III exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: datum defects, S-transformations, free networks and the congruency test. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Datum defect

Exercise 1: state the datum defect for (a) a GNSS network with no fixed station; (b) a 2D network of directions and distances; (c) a levelling network with one benchmark fixed; (d) a 3D terrestrial network with levelled instruments.

S-transformation for levelling

Exercise 2: E and S for a three-point levelling network with the datum on points 1 and 2. Exercise 4: transform the R1–R2–R3 datum displacements into the R2–R3 datum.

Two-epoch explorer: the Part III network

The running example: benchmarks R1, R2, R3 and stud S, six levelled lines, two epochs. Exercise 3: compare the precisions of the datums. Exercise 6: what if R2 also rose 4 mm?

Global congruency test

Exercise 8: dᵀQ⁺d = 3.2 mm² for a levelling network of 5 points, σ0 = 0.5 mm. Has it deformed at 95%?

All computations run in your browser. Critical values are computed from the χ² and F distributions, not looked up from tables.
