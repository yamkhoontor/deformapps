Part VII Solver

Calculators for the Part VII exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: collocation, total least squares, regularization and Bayesian estimation. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Least squares collocation along a line

Exercise 1: why is the prediction error at 80 m larger than at 50 m? Exercise 2: what is the correlation between points 30 m and 60 m apart? The data are the tunnel settlement points of Section 23.3.

Total least squares for a straight line

Exercise 3: Sxx = 50, Syy = 60, Sxy = 52. Compute the OLS and TLS slopes. Exercise 4: why does OLS underestimate the slope? Run the simulation.

Ridge regularization

Exercise 5: why does λ = 0.1 give consistent but wrong results? Exercise 6: which prior standard deviation corresponds to λ = 0.01 when σ0 = 0.01? The problem is the weak two-unknown example of Section 25.3.

Bayesian test of a benchmark

Exercise 7: d = +0.8 mm with σ = 0.43 mm; classical test and Bayes factor (τ = 5 mm). Exercise 8: what does holding a benchmark fixed mean as a prior?

All computations run in your browser. Critical values and probabilities are computed from the normal distribution, not looked up from tables.
