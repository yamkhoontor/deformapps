Part V Solver

Calculators for the Part V exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: congruency testing, unstable reference points, IWST, strain and tilt. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Can the epochs be pooled?

Exercise 1: two epochs give vᵀPv = 12.4 (r = 10) and 4.1 (r = 8). Can a pooled variance factor be used?

Global congruency test (F-test)

Exercise 2: a 2D network of 6 points gives Ω = 15.2 mm² with pooled s0² = 0.80 mm² and f = 20. Has it deformed at 95%?

Single-point elimination

Exercise 3: why was S removed before R1? Run the elimination on the Part III network (R1, R2, R3, S; six lines; two epochs).

IWST: a robust datum

Exercise 4: four benchmarks show (+2.1, +2.4, −3.0, +2.2) mm; find the IWST datum. Exercise 5: why are the first 2D iterations slow? Try the five-pillar network of Section 16.3.

Strain analysis

Exercise 6: εxx = −15 ppm, εyy = −5 ppm, εxy = 0. Compute the dilatation, maximum shear and principal strains. Or fit strain to displacements (Example 18.3).

Building tilt and local distortion

Exercise 7 and Example 18.5: fit a tilt plane to the settlements of studs S1–S6, test it, and find which stud departs from it.

All computations run in your browser. Critical values are computed from the χ² and F distributions, not looked up from tables.
