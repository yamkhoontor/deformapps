Part VI Solver

Calculators for the Part VI exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: kinematic models, Kalman filtering, cause–response models, time series and alarms. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Kinematic models: velocity and acceleration

Exercise 1: a constant-velocity fit gives T = 14.2 with r = 4; is it acceptable? Exercise 2: when will the settlement of S reach −30 mm? The data are the weekly settlements of S (Section 19.2).

Kalman filter for a settling point

Exercise 3: Φ and Q for Δt = 2 weeks. Exercises 4 and 5: why is week 7 flagged by the standard filter, and why is the robust filter dangerous? The data contain a booking error at week 6 and a real 4 mm step at week 11.

HST model for a dam crest

Exercise 6: why is the time coefficient (1.21 ± 0.04 mm/year) so precise when the readings vary by more than 7 mm each year? Switch terms on and off to see.

Correlated noise: effective number of observations

Exercise 7: 365 daily readings with lag-1 autocorrelation 0.8. How many independent observations are they worth?

Why the annual term must be estimated

Exercise 8: a station subsides 3 mm/year with an annual cycle of 4 mm. Fit the trend with and without the annual term, using noise-free data, and see the bias.

Traffic-light alarm check

Exercise 9: a scheme for the building next to the excavation, combining absolute, rate, statistical and geometric thresholds. Enter the latest values to see the status.

All computations run in your browser. Critical values are computed from the χ² and normal distributions, not looked up from tables.
