Part VIII Solver

Calculators for the Part VIII exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: sensors, network design, epoch frequency, tunnel convergence and statistical tables. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Level of detection for laser scanning (M3C2)

Exercise 1: roughness 4 mm, 30 points per cylinder in each epoch, registration error 1.5 mm. Compute the 95% level of detection.

InSAR: line of sight to vertical

Exercise 2: +5 mm line-of-sight displacement (towards the satellite) with incidence angle 35°, assuming purely vertical motion.

Pre-analysis of a levelling monitoring network

Exercise 3: why does Design A meet the precision requirement but still get rejected? Compare it with Design B (Section 28.2).

Monitoring interval

Exercise 4: alert 20 mm, action 30 mm, wall moving about 3 mm/week. What interval would you recommend?

Tunnel convergence: radius and ovalisation

Exercise 5: why must the centre offsets x0, y0 be estimated even though they are not deformation? The data are the ring of Section 29.3.

Statistical tables on demand

Exercise 6: find the critical value for a congruency test with h = 3 and pooled redundancy f = 20. Any value from Appendix A can be computed here.

All computations run in your browser. Critical values are computed from the distributions, not looked up from tables. Single-point elimination (Exercise 7) is in the Part V Solver.
