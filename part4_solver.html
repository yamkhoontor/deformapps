Part IV Solver

Calculators for the Part IV exercises of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis: reliability, outlier tests, L1 and robust estimation. Each opens with the exercise values; change any number to see the working update.

Minimal detectable bias

Exercise 1: an observation has σ = 1.0 mm and redundancy number 0.25. What is its minimal detectable bias with δ0 = 4.13?

Network analyzer: the Part IV levelling network

Benchmarks R1 (fixed at 10.000 m), R2, R3 and stud S; six levelled lines. Exercise 2: why is every ri = 0.5? Exercise 7: what defeats the two-blunder case? Inject blunders and switch lines on or off.

Baarda's w-test

Exercise 3: v = −1.8 mm, σ0 = 0.6 mm, qvv = 0.40. Carry out the w-test at α0 = 0.1%.

Robust weight functions

Exercise 4: Huber weights (k = 1.5) for u = 0.8, 1.5, 3.0, 6.0. Exercise 5: IGG III weights (k0 = 1.5, k1 = 3.0) for u = 1.2, 2.0, 2.5, 3.5.

Mean, median and breakdown

Exercise 6: seven readings with three blunders in the same direction. Which of the mean and the median is spoiled? What if there are four?

Smallest detectable movement

Exercise 8: a displacement has σd = 0.6 mm. What is the smallest movement detectable at 5% with 80% power?

All computations run in your browser. The L1 solution is found exactly by checking every subset of observations that fits the unknowns, rather than approximately.
