One Network Explorer

The teaching network of Deformation Monitoring and Analysis, three benchmarks and one stud, followed through every technique in the book: adjustment and datum, outliers, two-epoch deformation analysis and many-epoch monitoring. Change any data and every stage updates.

The network and its data

Four points on a construction site: deep benchmarks R1, R2, R3 and a settlement stud S on a building next to an excavation. All six height differences are levelled in each epoch with σ = 0.5 mm per line. These data feed every stage of the explorer.

Stage 1: adjustment and the datum

Adjust epoch 1. Change the datum and watch what changes (heights, precisions) and what does not (residuals, test statistics, redundancy). Switch lines off to see reliability fall. Parts II–IV and Appendix E.

Stage 2: outliers and robust estimation

Add a blunder to any line of epoch 1 (R1 fixed, as in Part IV). Compare data snooping with L1 and the M-estimators, and try two blunders to see masking.

Stage 3: two epochs — has anything moved?

Compare epochs 1 and 2. The datum changes every displacement but not the congruency test. Find the unstable benchmark by testing, by elimination, by the median (IWST) datum and by Bayesian reasoning. Parts III, V and VII.

Epoch data and true movements are set in tab 0.

Stage 4: many epochs — how is it moving?

After the two-epoch analysis, S is levelled weekly from the stable benchmarks. Test kinematic models, predict, and compare the standard, robust and adaptive Kalman filters on a booking error and a real step. Part VI.

All computations run in your browser, from the same data and methods as the book. Critical values are computed from the normal, χ² and F distributions.
